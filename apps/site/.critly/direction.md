# Direction

Subject: megha, an open-source ACP agent that runs T3 Code (and Zed) threads on Codex Cloud and Claude Code on the web. For developers who already use T3 and pay for ChatGPT or Claude.
Job: say what it is in one line, show a cloud thread playing out, and get the one-line setup prompt copied.
Kept: B, "the monsoon cloud": the headline sits inside a huge cloud bank drawn from the mark's circles, whose speech-bubble tail points down at a live thread demo. Also tried: A, a centered sans headline over a plain dark ground with the window below; it read clearly but could have been any dev tool, and the owner asked for clouds as the signature.
References: Stripe Payments (working UI right under the headline), monsoon miniature paintings (layered cloud banks, warm light under dark cloud, peacock blue), the T3 Code app itself (the thread layout people will recognize).

## Materials
- Type: Eczar 600 for h1/h2 (Rosetta's Latin + Devanagari face; its own Devanagari draws "मेघ"), Instrument Sans for body and UI, JetBrains Mono for prompts, logs, paths and diffs.
- Palette: storm night. Dark ground oklch(0.155 0.028 285), cloud layers 0.2 / 0.235 / 0.275 at hue 285-292, warm underlight oklch(0.62 0.11 55) at low opacity, ink oklch(0.955 0.012 85). Light is the washed sky after rain: sky oklch(0.88 0.03 255), white cloud bank, ink oklch(0.2 0.03 285). One accent, peacock blue: dark oklch(0.83 0.12 195) with dark text, light oklch(0.47 0.1 205) with white text. Green and red only in diffs and check marks.
- Shape: clouds are unions of circles (the mark's construction) with a flat base. 14px radius on windows, 10px on cards, pills for buttons and chips. 1px borders from --border; one deep shadow, only on the thread window.
- Imagery: the product, drawn in HTML: a T3 thread that plays from prompt to answer, labelled as an illustration. The flow diagram is the same clouds in small.
- Signature: the cloud with a speech-bubble tail. Full size in the hero (the tail points at the thread) and over the closing call to action; small in the nav, the diagram and the footer. Prompts rise to the cloud; progress comes back down as rain.
- Voice: plain, specific, honest about limits. Errors and money: plain and calm.
- Density and pace: persuade surface, comfortable. Showcase motion in the hero, the thread demo and the diagram (drift, rain, a timeline); everything people click is instant or under 200ms. Reduced motion: nothing drifts, the demo shows its finished state with a Play button.

## Standard
Layout, navigation, controls and states follow the platform and Critly's concepts.
